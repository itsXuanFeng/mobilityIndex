# Literature for the Category 1–3 methods

This note gives the published work behind the methods of [Category 1](category1.md), [Category 2](category2.md) and [Category 3](category3.md) that are not classic textbook methods. Most entries are from the last three years (2023–2026). A few older papers are kept where they are the origin of a method we use.

Many of our methods are our own design: the height rule, the off-axis correction, the radar energy `p_r4`, and the burst classifier. For those, the table gives the closest related work and says how ours differs. No published work found validates radar activity energy, bout structure or posture times against MoCap the way these notes do.

Every reference was checked against Europe PMC, Crossref or arXiv (title, authors, venue, DOI) on 2026-10-26. Where a description goes beyond the abstract, that is said in the entry.

## Methods and references

| Method (where used) | Literature reference |
|---|---|
| **Activity level and active / inactive time from radar energy** (Cat 1 §2, §7: `n_cells`, `p_r4` above a per-cell noise floor, a threshold per 1 s epoch) | Closest related work: García-Gutiérrez G, Aparicio-Esteve E, Ureña J, Villadangos-Carrizo JM, Jiménez-Martín A, García-Domínguez JJ. *A Practical Case of Monitoring Older Adults Using mmWave Radar and UWB.* Sensors 26(2):681, 2026. [doi:10.3390/s26020681](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26020681). They run an adaptive STA/LTA detector on an aggregated, normalized radar energy signal to classify micro- and macro-movements into non-sedentary episodes, in a senior residence (proof of concept, 3 users). The difference: their reference is not MoCap, and they don't estimate an energy measure. Our features and their leave-one-subject-out validation are our own. |
| **Bouts: gap filling (interruption tolerance) + minimum bout duration** (Cat 2 §1) | Hibbing PR, Creasy SA, Carlson JA. *CRIB: A Novel Method for Device-Based Physical Behavior Analysis.* J Meas Phys Behav 5(4):277–287, 2022. [doi:10.1123/jmpb.2021-0059](https://doi.org/10.1123/jmpb.2021-0059). Compares interruption-tolerant bout algorithms and proposes a clustering-based one with limits on the number and duration of interruptions. Earlier evidence that how interruptions are handled changes bout counts and durations: Ayabe M et al. *Interruption in physical activity bout analysis: an accelerometry research issue.* BMC Res Notes 7:284, 2014. [doi:10.1186/1756-0500-7-284](https://doi.org/10.1186/1756-0500-7-284). Our choice of rule by calibrating it against the labels (leave-person-out) is our own. |
| **Fragmentation index = active → inactive transition probability (ASTP), on the raw epochs** (Cat 2 §2) | Definition and older-adult use: Schrack JA, Kuo P-L, Wanigatunga AA, Di J, Simonsick EM, Spira AP, Ferrucci L, Zipunnikov V. *Active-to-Sedentary Behavior Transitions, Fatigability, and Physical Functioning in Older Adults.* J Gerontol A 74(4):560–567, 2019. [doi:10.1093/gerona/gly243](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/gly243). The set of fragmentation metrics (ASTP = 1 / mean active bout duration, among ten metrics): Di J, Leroux A, Urbanek J, Varadhan R, Spira AP, Schrack J, Zipunnikov V. *Patterns of sedentary and active time accumulation are associated with mortality in US adults: The NHANES study.* bioRxiv, 2017. [doi:10.1101/182337](https://doi.org/10.1101/182337). Origin of the state-transition approach: Lim AS et al. Sleep 34(11):1569–1581, 2011. [doi:10.5665/sleep.1400](https://doi.org/10.5665/sleep.1400). |
| ASTP: recent use and properties (Cat 2 §2) | Popelsky BK, Pettee Gabriel K, Dooley EE, Ylitalo KR. *Physical Activity Fragmentation and Falls in Older Adults: Findings From the National Health and Aging Trends Study.* J Gerontol A 79(7):glae129, 2024. [doi:10.1093/gerona/glae129](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glae129). Lamoureux NR, Saavedra JM, Downer B, Schrack JA, Wanigatunga AA. *Physical Activity Fragmentation and Functional Decline among Older Adults: Findings from NHATS.* J Gerontol A 81(9):glag177, 2026. [doi:10.1093/gerona/glag177](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glag177). Mathematical properties and estimators of the transition probability: Danilevicz IM, van Hees VT, van der Heide FCT, Jacob L, Landré B, Benadjaoud MA, Sabia S. *Measures of fragmentation of rest activity patterns: mathematical properties and interpretability based on accelerometer real life data.* BMC Med Res Methodol 24:132, 2024. [doi:10.1186/s12874-024-02255-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12874-024-02255-w). All of these use wrist or hip accelerometers at minute level; none of them uses radar or 1 s epochs. |
| **Posture from the top-of-body height of a ceiling radar point cloud** (Cat 3 §3–4: height rule with three thresholds) | Closest benchmark design: Garg S, Černý M, Hrubý D, Bansal A. *RadarMoCap-Posture: A Synchronized mmWave Point-Cloud and Motion-Capture Benchmark for Human Posture Recognition.* Zenodo dataset, 2026. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.22945438](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22945438). A single ceiling-mounted TI IWR6843 in a mock nursing-home room, with markerless MoCap as the reference; lying, sitting, standing and walking; 13 adults aged 21–65. Learned posture classifiers on point clouds: Zhang G, Li S, Zhang K, Lin YJ. Sensors 23(16):7208, 2023. [doi:10.3390/s23167208](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23167208); Abdullah S, Ahmed S, Choi C, Cho SH. Sensors 24(22):7250, 2024. [doi:10.3390/s24227250](https://doi.org/10.3390/s24227250). Our rule is our own: 90th-percentile height near the person, with slow Doppler bins kept so that a still person stays visible. |
| Height of the point cloud as a posture / fall cue (Cat 3 §4) | Jin F, Sengupta A, Cao S. *mmFall: Fall Detection Using 4-D mmWave Radar and a Hybrid Variational RNN AutoEncoder.* IEEE Trans Autom Sci Eng 19(2):1245–1257, 2022. [doi:10.1109/TASE.2020.3042158](https://doi.org/10.1109/TASE.2020.3042158): a fall is a drop in centroid height together with a motion-anomaly spike. Chen H-H, Lin J-D, Lin S-H, Wu C-W, Chen H-C. *Millimeter-wave technology for multi-person fall detection validated through wearable sensors and real-life scenarios.* Sci Rep 16:8859, 2026. [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-40330-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-40330-y): tracks the point-cloud centre; a web-search summary of the full text describes a low-height + fast-descent trigger confirmed by stillness, which is not in the abstract. |
| **Still person on the floor** (Cat 3 Bottleneck 3: the floor posture is mostly carried forward) | Trinh H, Thai P, Creager E, Shaker G. *Reliable Quasi-Static Post-Fall Floor-Occupancy Detection Using Low-Cost Millimetre-Wave Radar.* arXiv:2601.17710, 2026 (preprint). Capon/MVDR beamforming + CA-CFAR to detect a near-motionless person on the floor; 7 participants in a setting like a long-term care room. A possible next step for the floor. |
| **Off-axis height bias and its correction** (Cat 3 §4: *k*·*d*) | Ataman FC, Kumar Y.B. C, Rao S, Ozev S. *Improving Angle of Arrival Estimation Accuracy for mm-Wave Radars.* IEEE International Test Conference (ITC), 30–36, 2023. [doi:10.1109/ITC51656.2023.00013](https://doi.org/10.1109/ITC51656.2023.00013). Systematic azimuth / elevation errors that grow away from boresight, with corrections. This is a different cause from ours: our direction cosines are unbiased, and the bias comes from which body part is seen. The linear correction is our own. |
| **Detecting postural transitions in radar Doppler; burst classifier** (Cat 3 §6, comparison methods) | Saho K, Uemura K, Fujimoto M, Matsumoto M. *Evaluation of Higher-Level Instrumental Activities of Daily Living via Micro-Doppler Radar Sensing of Sit-to-Stand-to-Sit Movement.* IEEE J Transl Eng Health Med 8:2100211, 2020. [doi:10.1109/JTEHM.2020.2964209](https://doi.org/10.1109/JTEHM.2020.2964209): ceiling 24 GHz Doppler radar, older adults aged 65–74. Saho K, Sugano K, Uemura K, Matsumoto M. *Screening of apathetic elderly adults using kinematic information in gait and sit-to-stand/stand-to-sit movements measured with Doppler radar.* Health Informatics J, 2021. [doi:10.1177/1460458221990051](https://doi.org/10.1177/1460458221990051). Kobak J, Richman BJ, Washington L Jr, Hamza SA. *Continuous Human Activity Recognition using a MIMO Radar for Transitional Motion Analysis.* arXiv:2304.06173, 2023 (preprint): STA/LTA segmentation of micro-Doppler, then a CNN. Our burst features and the leave-person-out gradient boosting are our own. |
| Real-world radar detection of sit-down / stand-up (Cat 3, context for the counts) | Jayabahu D, Siva P. *Dataset for Real-World Human Action Detection Using FMCW mmWave Radar.* arXiv:2412.17517, 2024 (to appear in Proc. JCVIS). 28 older adults' homes, TI IWR6843AOP point clouds, more than 900 chair transfers; a baseline CNN reaches only recall 0.55 and precision 0.13 in real homes. |
| Sit-to-stand analysis with mmWave radar, against reference systems (Cat 3 §9) | Hu S, Ackun P, Zhang X, Cao S, Barton J, Hector MG, Fain MJ, Toosizadeh N. *mmWave Radar for Sit-to-Stand Analysis: A Comparative Study With Wearables and Kinect.* IEEE Trans Biomed Eng 72(9):2623–2634, 2025. [doi:10.1109/TBME.2025.3548092](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2025.3548092). 60 GHz point clouds, pose model + inverse kinematics, 45 participants, Kinect and wearables as the reference. |
| **Peak radial (Doppler) speed of a transition as a metric** (Cat 3 §7) | Ziaee Bideskan M, Karbaschi N, Abedi H, Abbasi Z. *Radar-Based Assessment of Sit-to-Stand Transitions as Digital Biomarkers of Pain and Physical Decline.* Sensors 26(9):2769, 2026. [doi:10.3390/s26092769](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26092769): 60 GHz FMCW, peak ascent velocity of the torso across mounting heights and speeds. Why sit-to-stand velocity matters for ageing: O'Brien MW. *Time to stand up faster: underutilization of real-world sit-to-stand transition velocity in aging research.* Front Aging 7:1639286, 2026. [doi:10.3389/fragi.2026.1639286](https://doi.org/10.3389/fragi.2026.1639286). |
| **Power-weighted quantile of the Doppler spectrum as the peak speed** (Cat 3 §7: `v_q950` instead of the fastest cell) | This is the *percentile method* for micro-Doppler envelopes: the upper envelope is the velocity below which a fixed fraction (typically 95–97%) of the power of each time slice lies. Origin: van Dorp P, Groen FCA. *Feature-based human motion parameter estimation with radar.* IET Radar Sonar Navig 2(2):135–145, 2008. [doi:10.1049/iet-rsn:20070086](https://doi.org/10.1049/iet-rsn:20070086). Older than three years, but it is the established name and reference for what `v_q*` computes. We apply it to the person's points only, and we calibrate the quantile leave-person-out. |
| Posture classification from ceiling radar, leave-one-subject-out (Cat 3 §9; posture times) | Lu W, Bird C, Sandhu M, Silvera-Tawil D. *Office Posture Detection Using Ceiling-Mounted Ultra-Wideband Radar and Attention-Based Modality Fusion.* Sensors 25(16):5164, 2025. [doi:10.3390/s25165164](https://doi.org/10.3390/s25165164). UWB rather than FMCW; short scripted trials. No paper found validates the radar's time spent standing, sitting, lying and on the floor against a reference. |

## Classic methods, not searched further

These are standard methods with a standard reference; they are listed so it is clear they weren't overlooked.
